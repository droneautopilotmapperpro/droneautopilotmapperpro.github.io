SKILL 02TAB: SETUP & SDK

Multi-Battery Split & Resume WPT #Smart Multi-Battery Split & Resume WPT #

Membagi misi besar menjadi leg B1..BN sesuai reserve RTH (10–50%), overlap 1 WPT, serta batas otomatis ≤ 99 WPT di MSDK V4. Splits large surveys into B1..BN battery legs respecting RTH reserve, 1-WPT overlap continuity & MSDK V4 99-WPT cap.
SETUP/SDK → Pilih chip B1/B2 atau isi Resume WPT # sebelum Upload / Deploy KMZ.
SKILL 03TAB: CAM

Target GSD Solver & Motion-Blur GuardTarget GSD Solver & Motion-Blur Guard

Hitung ketinggian akurat dari target GSD (1.0–5.0 cm/px) & jaga foto bebas blur dengan tombol instan Apply Safe Speed. Solves exact altitude from target GSD (1.0–5.0 cm/px) and guards frame smear against shutter speed with 1-tap Safe Speed.
CAM → Pilih pill GSD → pastikan status SHARP (tekan Apply Safe Speed bila kuning/merah).
SKILL 04TAB: CAM

Smart Flight-Angle Optimizer (0°–180°)Smart Flight-Angle Auto-Optimizer (0°–180°)

Menyapu azimut tiap 5° di poligon miring untuk mencari sudut dengan belokan tersedikit & waktu tercepat (plus input angka kustom). Sweeps azimuth in 5° steps on skewed polygons to cut turns and flight duration (plus exact custom angle control).
Gambar poligon miring → tab CAM → tekan Optimize Angle ⚡.
SKILL 05TAB: DEM

Profil Elevasi 2D & Clearance Terrain2D Mission Elevation & Clearance Chart

Grafik Canvas langsung di tab DEM: kurva kontur tanah vs lintasan terbang, garis aman AGL, MIN AGL, puncak bukit & slope maksimum. Real-time Canvas chart comparing terrain contour vs aircraft altitude, safety AGL floor, peak ground elevation & max climb slope.
Tab DEM → Load DEM (.tif) → Apply DEM to WPTs → cek grafik agar bebas titik merah (<20m AGL).
SKILL 06TAB: SETUP (PRO)

No-Fly Zone (NFZ) Keep-Out & Auto-CarveNo-Fly Zone (NFZ) Keep-Out Cutout & Audit

Tambahkan zona halangan (SUTET, tower, crane). Engine memotong jalur grid otomatis dengan geometri JTS & memblokir bila melanggar NFZ. Carves clean obstacle keep-out zones (towers, power lines, restricted sites) using JTS geometry and blocks accidental breaches.
SETUP → Pro Tools > NFZ → atur Radius + Buffer → + Circle @ Map Center lalu Calculate Plan.
SKILL 07TAB: SETUP (3D ORBIT)

3D Orbit Helikal & Inspeksi POI Menara3D Helical Orbit & Tower POI Inspector

Buat misi orbit melingkar/spiral multi-cincin dengan hidung drone terkunci ke pusat objek (Toward POI WPML) & tilt gimbal otomatis. Generates multi-ring circular or helical climbs with nose locked inward (Toward POI in WPML) & automatic oblique gimbal angle.
Pusatkan peta pada gedung/tower → SETUP > Pro Tools > 3D ORBIT → Set @ Map Center → Generate.
SKILL 08TAB: SETUP (WIND)

Hitung vektor *wind triangle* per segmen, penalti durasi baterai, peringatan *stall*, serta saran sudut jalur silang-angin terbaik. Computes per-leg wind triangles, battery endurance penalty, stall limits & recommended crosswind survey azimuth.
SETUP > Pro Tools > WIND SIM → isi kec. & arah angin → lihat rekomendasi azimut silang angin.
SKILL 09TAB: SDK

Skor Go/No-Go 0–100% otomatis memeriksa 10 gerbang keselamatan: sinkronisasi plan, batas WPT V4/V5, GNSS, baterai, plafon, DEM, NFZ, angin & blur. Automated 0–100% avionics scorecard auditing freshness, WPT cap, GNSS lock, battery, ceiling, DEM, NFZ, wind & blur.
Buka tab SDK → cek kartu Pre-Flight Safety Gate hingga berstatus GO FOR FLIGHT.
